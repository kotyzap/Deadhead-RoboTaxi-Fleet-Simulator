0.34.1 — Clock off jumps straight to 06:00, no more "Advancing…"

Pavel: after Clock off the topbar sat on "Advancing…" for a few real
seconds while the overnight fast-forward played out, with the speed/pause
segment next to it still showing a highlighted button as if it were live.
Nothing happens overnight worth watching — cars are parked, at most
finishing a charge — so the animation was just a delay with a confusing
control sitting next to it, not a paced moment.

WHAT CHANGED

startFastForward() no longer hands the overnight gap to the tick interval
to drip-feed at CFG.ffwdSimPerReal (7200x) over several real seconds. It
now calls step(target-S.t) once, synchronously, then stopFastForward()
immediately after — same tick-by-tick math (billMidnight, per-car
charging, the day counter, bankruptcy's early exit) as before, just run
back-to-back in one JS call instead of spread across real-time frames.
S.ffwd/S.ffwdTarget are still set for the instant that call is on the
stack, so clockOn()'s guard and restore()'s defensive reset still mean
something — but no render() ever happens while S.ffwd reads true any
more, so the "Advancing…" topbar text and the disabled clock button are
now vestigial rather than something a player can see. Left in as a
safety net (documented at both call sites) rather than deleted, same
treatment as the tick loop's now-dead-in-practice `if(S.ffwd)` branch.

Verified with a throwaway jsdom script (clockOn → clockOff with the game
at 23:26): the whole jump to next 06:00 completes inside the clockOff()
call itself (elapsed real time <500ms, no interval ticks involved), S.t
lands exactly on nextDayStart(), S.day advances, and render() afterward
shows the normal "Clock on" button rather than a stuck "Advancing…".
Existing suite (8 files) and check-parity still pass; nothing in it
exercised the old animated path, so nothing needed updating there.

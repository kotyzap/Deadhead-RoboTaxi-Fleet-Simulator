0.24.0 — Miami, and the city gate becomes a chain

City #3 is Miami, chosen over Houston (same-day Dallas launch, same rules — a
bronze re-skin) and Orlando/Tampa (wants a demand curve that doesn't exist
yet). Tesla's real 3 July 2026 geofence is 10–14 sq mi of WESTERN Miami-Dade
and excludes everything Miami is famous for: no downtown, no Brickell, no
Miami Beach, no MIA. That exclusion list is the scenario.

- First city with NO airport zone. The policy strip's airport toggle now
  disables itself and says why; before this it stayed live and did nothing.
- First city with fareK below 1 (0.94) — no CBD, no airport premium.
- FPL's summer peak runs noon–21:00 at 26c/kWh: nine expensive hours where
  Austin has five. Published tariff, not a difficulty knob.
- insK 1.35, the worst in the table. Legacy 72 kW charging, mostly outside
  the box. Busiest opener in the game at 20.2 offers/h.
- Chargers transcribed from tesla.com; zone centroids placed from street
  geography and labelled as such.

New mechanic: incident risk is a function, not the inline 0.00018 constant.
incidentRisk() = INC_BASE × cityK('incK') × weatherIncidentMult(), so rain
finally matters to safety (storm ×2.2) and a city can be rougher than another
(Miami incK 1.30). The elevated risk is STATED in the Incidents panel rather
than merely applied.

Gate chain: needs:'shift1@<city>'. One Dallas shift opens Miami, one Austin
shift opens Dallas. The bare 'shift1' read the live run only and could not
tell which city the shift happened in, so a second Austin shift would have
opened city #3. Adds shiftFinished(), cityShiftDone(), progNoteShift() and
PROG.results[city].shiftDone; PROG_V 1→2 with a migration that infers the flag
from day>1 rather than making existing players re-earn it.

Night accents now clear WCAG AA 4.5:1 as fills (Austin 3.60 → 4.55, Dallas
3.34 → 4.59, Miami 4.56). The old 3.0 floor was a deviation whose comment
defended it; --accent is a solid fill under white text, so the dark surface
behind it was never relevant. Gradient top stops get a 4.0 floor.

Also: deploy/scripts/nudge-zones.js snaps zone centroids onto routable roads
via OSRM /nearest (up to 452 m off today), with nudge-splice.test.js covering
the splice offline. DESIGN.md §5 corrected (real launch dates, Dallas's
compact geofence, Miami's exclusion list) and §6.1 (real time scale:
simPerReal 1, speeds 0/1/4/20).

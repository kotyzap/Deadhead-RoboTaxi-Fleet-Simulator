0.46.0 — Paolo notices a dead board

WHAT CHANGED

Clock on with no ride service connected and, ten REAL seconds later, Paolo
speaks: which service to pick, what each takes, and that the riders score you
afterwards even though the car did the driving.

WHY IT NEEDED ITS OWN TRIGGER

nextTask() has always returned the 'plat' task for an unconnected fleet, so the
objective line said "Connect a platform" — but the only thing that said it OUT
LOUD was rayAdvise(), and that fires after ADVISE_AFTER (75) *sim*-seconds of
the world being quiet. For a player clocked on with no feed, quiet is not a
signal, it is the permanent condition: nothing will ever happen, so the advisor
is waiting for a change that cannot arrive. At 1x the advice was over a minute
away, and a minute of a screen where nothing moves reads as a broken game.

TIMED IN REAL SECONDS

noFeedTick() takes wall-clock seconds and is called from the tick interval
OUTSIDE the S.ffwd / S.running branches — deliberately, on both counts:

  - Sim-seconds would arrive in half a second at 20x and never at 1x, which is
    exactly backwards for a nudge about the player's own setup. Same reasoning
    as the offer countdown and the blocked-car window (see stepOnce's realDt).
  - step() is skipped entirely at speed 0, so a counter living inside it would
    stall for a player who clocked on and then paused to look around.

The clock only runs while the mistake is being made and resets the moment a
feed comes on, so connecting at second nine means the card never appears.

ONCE PER SHIFT

S.ray.feedShift records the shift he last warned about it, not a boolean:
clocking on to a dead board is worth catching every time it happens, and a
player who does it on day 3 has forgotten day 1. Default is -1, never 0 — 0 is
a real shift number and would silence the nudge for a fleet that has not worked
yet. Silent during the guided tutorial (which teaches platforms in its own
order) and for a player who skipped Paolo. S.noFeedSec is transient: never
trusted from a save, or restoring mid-count would fire him on resume.

The commission, the demand share and the service names are all read from
PLATFORMS rather than written into the copy — a rebalance or a third feed must
not turn the speech into a lie (the achievements funnel's hardcoded city list
is the precedent).

TESTS

test/nofeed-nudge.test.js (new): the ten-second wait, the reset on connecting in
time, silence off the clock, once-per-shift, silence during the tutorial and
after a skip, and that the copy quotes the live PLATFORMS numbers and mentions
ratings.

0.55.0 — Paolo pauses the sim while he's talking, and stays quiet in the garage

WHAT WAS WRONG

Two asks from Pavel (2026-07-30):

  1. Every Paolo card already required a button click to go away — rayFoot()
     always sets both #ray-go and #ray-skip, and #ray is deliberately "never
     modal" (no Escape, no outside-click dismiss). But nothing stopped the
     clock while a card sat on screen: offers, rides, incidents and charging
     all kept moving underneath it, so reading a longer beat could cost real
     game state.
  2. Nothing suppressed Paolo while the garage was open. Beat 1 (his intro)
     is meant to fire there — it's opened directly by openGarage() — but
     every OTHER trigger (the other 13 scripted beats, the ambient advisor,
     the no-feed nudge, first-decline, a new city unlocking, the dodge
     brief) could also fire while the garage modal was covering the screen,
     landing a card the player couldn't act on properly (raySpot() already
     had to special-case "target is behind the garage" for exactly this).

WHAT CHANGED

Pause: two small helpers, rayPause()/rayResume(), following the exact
prevSpeed/setSpeed(0) pattern openDodge()/closeDodge() already used for the
mini-game. Every one of the 8 places a card can appear (rayShow, rayAdvise,
the no-feed nudge, rayDodgeBrief, rayFirstDecline, rayCityUnlocked,
rayCoffee, rayClockOffWarn) now ends with rayPause(); both universal
dismissal paths (rayDismiss(), raySkip()) now start with rayResume(). Cards
that chain synchronously (dismissing one can immediately trigger the next
via rayCheck()) still end up paused at the correct ORIGINAL speed — resume
runs before the next card's own pause re-captures it, all in one synchronous
call stack, so nothing is ever painted in between and there's no flicker.

openDodge() needed one adjustment: "Take control" opens directly out of the
dodge-brief card, which has already zeroed S.speed via rayPause(). openDodge()
now adopts that pause (reads RAY_PREV_SPEED instead of the already-zeroed
S.speed, and clears the pause flag itself) rather than capturing 0 as
DODGE.prevSpeed, which would have resumed the sim mid-mini-game the moment
rayDismiss() ran right after.

Garage suppression: a `$('garage').hidden` guard added to rayCheck() (covers
all 13 non-beat-1 scripted beats plus guideCheck()'s forced day-1 ones),
rayAdvise(), noFeedTick(), and the call sites for rayFirstDecline(),
rayCityUnlocked(), rayClockOffWarn(), and both rayDodgeBrief() triggers (the
Camera nav icon, and clicking a blocked-car alert directly). rayCoffee() is
deliberately left unguarded — it only ever fires from a direct settings-menu
click, which the garage's own modal already makes unreachable while open.
Beat 1 stays reachable exactly as before, since openGarage() calls rayShow(1)
directly rather than through rayCheck().

TESTS

New test/ray-pause-garage.test.js (26 checks): a card zeros S.speed/S.running
and remembers the interrupted speed; both "Got it" and "Skip" restore it
(Skip still sets S.ray.skipped, unchanged); a chained beat-1-into-beat-2
sequence keeps the speed from BEFORE either card, not the 0 the first one
left behind; the dodge mini-game's own DODGE.prevSpeed is the true original
speed, not 0, and stays paused through rayDismiss() rather than being
resumed out from under it; beat 1 still fires while the garage is open;
rayCheck() and rayAdvise() fire nothing else while it's open; both work
again the moment it closes.

Full suite passes clean (`npm test`, 431 checks — 405 previous + 26 new),
and `check-parity` is green — deploy/public/index.html is byte-identical to
deadhead.html.

0.63.0 — Landscape intro: video beside the form, sized to actually fill the screen

WHAT WAS WRONG

Pavel's reports (2026-07-30), on a wide-but-short (landscape) window:

  1. "position form below the title of the image. video on the left,
     forms on the right. We need to see Title of the image." The stacked
     layout's total height is video-height + form-height; on a landscape
     window that total routinely exceeded the viewport, and #intro's own
     vertical centering pushed the frame up over the splash art's own
     baked-in title.
  2. Relaxed a moment later: "use the size of the display to make video
     bigger. Forms can be below or on the side" — the video itself was
     also sized off the AVAILABLE WIDTH only, which on a landscape window
     (wide, short) is the wrong axis to size from; it left the video
     smaller than the screen actually had room for while still being tall
     enough to crowd out the title above it.
  3. "it was really hard to pause the video" — almost certainly the
     autoplay-with-sound added in 0.62.0: a video that starts paused shows
     its native controls at rest; one that starts PLAYING has its native
     controls auto-hide after a couple of seconds of no mouse movement,
     per ordinary browser behaviour, which is not something CSS or JS can
     override for the native <video controls> UI. Noted below rather than
     "fixed" — there is no code bug here to point at, only a trade-off
     that shipped with autoplay.

WHAT CHANGED

The two real asks turned out to be the same fix. A new landscape media
query (`orientation:landscape` and at least 700px wide, so a genuinely
narrow landscape phone stays on the existing stacked layout) turns
.intro-frame into a row: the video on the left, and a new .intro-right
wrapper (holding the account card + Play-on-this-device button, previously
direct children of .intro-frame) as a fixed-width column on the right.
Side by side, the frame's total height is the TALLER of the two halves
rather than their sum — shorter than stacked by a wide margin, which is
what actually clears the title.

#intro-video is now sized off viewport HEIGHT in that same media query
(66vh, capped at 680px; 58vh/560px on a short window under 560px tall)
rather than width — on a landscape screen height is almost always the
scarcer axis, so sizing from it means "bigger video" and "room for the
title" stop being competing goals. A taller landscape window hands the
video more room automatically, the same reasoning the width-based sizing
already used for portrait.

.intro-right is a plain flex column (display:flex;flex-direction:column;
gap:18px) unconditionally, so the DEFAULT stacked layout looks pixel-
identical to before the wrapper existed — .intro-frame's own gap still
separates the video from the whole block, and .intro-right's own gap
supplies what used to be the direct gap between the account card and the
button. Only the landscape query gives it a fixed 340px width and centres
it vertically. The 30px push added to the account card in 0.62.0 (for
separating it from a video stacked directly above) is zeroed out in
landscape, where .intro-right is already vertically centred as its own
column and the extra offset just left it sitting oddly low.

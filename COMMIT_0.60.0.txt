0.60.0 — Intro screen cleanup: drop the redundant title, lighten the scrim, fix the Skip button's contrast

WHAT WAS WRONG

Three asks from Pavel (2026-07-30), all about the sign-in/registration
screen that now lands directly on the splash artwork (v0.58.0):

  1. "From the registration form remove DEADHEAD. Use top of the picture."
     The intro frame carried its own "DEADHEAD / Robotaxi fleet simulator"
     title above the video, duplicating the wordmark that already sits near
     the top of the splash photo behind it.
  2. "Also remove the black background around the form so it is not that
     dark." Two dark layers were stacking on top of each other: #intro's
     own scrim over the photo (rgba(6,7,9,.5)) plus .intro-frame's own
     plate behind the video/form (rgba(10,12,16,.62)) — between the two,
     the photo barely showed through and the whole screen read as black.
  3. "Color also the Play on this device only. It is hidden black on
     black." #intro-skip used the same near-transparent white-on-dark
     treatment as #intro-start, which works fine on #intro-start's
     accent-lit .pri variant but left #intro-skip almost unreadable on its
     own over a dark photo.

WHAT CHANGED

The title is gone. .intro-brand's markup and CSS are removed outright, not
just hidden — nothing else referenced the class. The splash art is
unaffected; it was already CONTAIN-fit specifically so its own DEADHEAD
title is never cropped (see the .splash-bg comment from the splash
rework), so the photo's own title is what carries the name now.

The scrim: #intro.intro-onsplash's background dropped from rgba(6,7,9,.5)
to rgba(6,7,9,.22), and .intro-frame's own plate dropped from
rgba(10,12,16,.62) to rgba(10,12,16,.38). Both layers were darkening the
same photo at once; lightening just one would have left the other
carrying all the contrast. The blur+saturate on both stay as they were —
this only touches how much black sits under them.

#intro-skip: recoloured from rgba(255,255,255,.08) fill / white text to a
solid white fill (#fff) with dark text (#0b0c0f), hover dims to
rgba(255,255,255,.85). #intro-start is untouched — it sits on the accent
colour via its .pri class and was never the button in question.

TESTS

Full suite re-run clean (test/intro-video.test.js in particular, since it
asserts on #intro's markup/CSS, passed with no changes needed). version.test.js
failed only on the expected "VERSION is ahead of the newest commit note"
check until this file was written.

Full suite passes clean, and check-parity is green — deploy/public/index.html
is byte-identical to deadhead.html.

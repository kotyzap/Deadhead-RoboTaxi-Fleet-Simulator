0.53.1 — the topbar fits one line when one line fits

WHAT WAS WRONG

Pavel, with three screenshots: a 2196px window rendering two lines with obvious
room to spare, and a narrower one rendering THREE ("we definitely do not need 3
lines of the top menu").

Two separate causes.

1. .tb-ctrls carried flex:1 0 100%. That is not "wrap when you have to" — it is
   "always break", at every width below 2250px. The row could physically fit on
   one line from about 1650px upward and never did. The >=2250px media query
   existed solely to undo it, and that threshold was measured against five city
   tabs and a Permit readout: eight tabs have shipped since, Permit and Coffee
   are gone, and theme/sound/units collapsed into one gear, so the number was
   stale in several directions at once and the row stayed on two lines well past
   the point it needed to.

2. .tb-info's column gap was 22px at EVERY width, and the block holds six items
   (brand, city strip, Day, Clock, Cash, Trophies) — five gaps, 110px of pure
   spacing, more than a whole readout. Past a certain narrowness that pushed
   Cash onto a second line INSIDE the block, and with the controls forced onto a
   line of their own the row became three deep.

WHAT CHANGED

.tb-ctrls is flex:0 1 auto and the 2250px query is deleted. The browser decides:
one line whenever both groups fit, two when they don't, at any width, with no
magic number to go stale.

This is only safe because of 0.53.0. The forced break existed to stop the wrap
landing somewhere arbitrary back when the row was a flat list of seventeen
items, and the comment defending it has been there since v0.26.3. But the row
has exactly TWO children now — Coffee was the third and moved into the gear menu
last release — so the only seam a natural wrap can pick is precisely the one the
forced break was guaranteeing. The protection became free, so the mechanism
could go.

.tb-info's gaps now step down with the window: 22px base, 14px at <=1699,
11px at <=1499. Gaps are the cheapest thing in that block to give up — nothing
is hidden and nothing shrinks, the row just stops being generous.

WHAT IS NOT CLAIMED

Whether the narrow case lands on two lines rather than three depends on the
exact window width and on real text metrics, and jsdom has no layout engine, so
this could not be measured here — only reasoned about with a width budget
(which puts the full row at ~1630px of a 2196px window, comfortably one line,
and says the gap change buys 32-44px in the block). The narrow screenshot's
actual CSS width is ambiguous from the image alone. If three lines survive at
some width, the next lever is the version chip in .brand or an earlier step on
the city-tab padding — both cheaper than hiding a number.

TESTS

city.test.js: the Coffee-mechanism check drops the two clauses that asserted the
forced break and the 2250px query (both now deleted). Two new checks — the
controls rule uses flex:0 1 auto with no 100% basis anywhere in it and no
min-width:2250px query survives in the file (a reintroduced 100% basis looks
harmless and would silently pin every window back to two lines), and the
.tb-info gap steps down at both tiers.

Full suite passes clean (405 checks).

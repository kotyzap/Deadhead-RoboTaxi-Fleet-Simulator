0.45.0 — the two-car opening

WHAT CHANGED

1. "Close" in the garage header is now "Leave Garage". Close reads as
   dismissing a dialog; the player is walking out of a garage with a car.
   It also becomes the primary-styled action (#gar-close.go) once something is
   in service, and stays a quiet dismiss while the fleet is empty — the modal
   is a forced decision until then.

2. The opening fleet may be ONE OR TWO cars. Before this, it was exactly one,
   and the rule was invisible: acquire() hid #garage the moment the first car
   landed, so "one is the limit" was indistinguishable from "the modal closed".
   Now:
     - OPENING_CAP = 2, applied by effCap() while S.shiftNo === 0.
     - effCap() only ever LOWERS the city's own fleetCap, never raises it, so
       SF's soloSeat cap of 1 is untouched.
     - acquire() closes the garage at the ceiling rather than at car #1, so the
       second slot is discoverable instead of taken away.

3. A slots strip (#gar-slots) makes the second slot visible: one chip per
   starting slot, filled chips naming the car and how it is held, the empty one
   dashed in accent and labelled "2nd vehicle — optional". Accent, not red —
   nothing is wrong with stopping at one. It hides itself after the first shift,
   where a row of 17 chips would say nothing.

4. Refusing a third during the opening names the opening rule ("Two vehicles is
   the most you can start with — clock on, then the garage opens up") instead of
   Austin's cap of 17, which would have been a lie.

5. The modal title tracks the same distinction the Fleet button already did:
   "Choose a vehicle" with no car, "Add a vehicle" after.

TRAP AVOIDED

Ray's beat 3 (spotlight #panel-platforms) triggered on "a car exists". That was
safe only because the first purchase closed the garage. With the garage staying
open, the beat would have fired from behind the scrim — and raySpot() refuses to
ring a target covered by an open modal, so it would have dimmed the screen
around nothing while talking about a panel the player cannot see. rayReady(3)
now also waits for the garage to be closed.

TESTS

- test/opening-fleet.test.js (new): the ceiling, the garage staying open, the
  slots copy, the button label, the SF one-car case, and the beat-3 gate.
- test/city.test.js: the cumulative-rent-burn check now sets S.shiftNo = 1. It
  rents until the burn gate bites, which is well past two cars — the opening cap
  would have stopped the loop early and tested the wrong rule.

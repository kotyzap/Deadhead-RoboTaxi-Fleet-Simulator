0.59.0 — Recenter frames the zones only, and the zone circles are visible

WHAT WAS WRONG

Three asks from Pavel (2026-07-30):

  1. "RECENTRE rename to US RECENTER" — the button read "Recentre", and its
     aria-label and both of setFollow()'s dynamic titles said "recentres".
  2. "when clicked only area around zones (circles) is recentered. Not the
     chargers." fleetBounds() fitted cars + active zones + chargers.
  3. "Also make all circles more visible. some are really light, almost
     invisible." Zone circles were created with `stroke:false` and a fill
     floor of 0.02.

WHAT CHANGED

Spelling: the label, the aria-label, the static title and BOTH strings
setFollow() writes are now US-spelled. Only player-facing text moved —
`recenterMap`, `#dh-recenter` and `#i-recenter` were already US-spelled, and
source comments were left alone (the file's prose is British throughout;
changing it would be a diff with no reader).

The box: fleetBounds() now fits active zones and nothing else. Chargers were
the actual complaint and the obvious cut — every city deliberately places at
least one well outside the demand area (Austin's Research Blvd is 27 minutes
out, and that distance is the whole point of it), so a box containing them
framed mostly empty ground with the operating area shrunk into a corner.
Cars came out for the same reason rather than as a separate decision: a car
dispatched to one of those far chargers, or on a flatbed after running flat,
would drag the box straight back out a tick later — so the frame would
wander for reasons that have nothing to do with where the work is. Zones are
fixed per city, which makes the box stable, which is what makes a 30-second
auto-recenter unobtrusive instead of a camera that lurches. Everything else
is still on the map, just off-screen until panned to. The all-zones-off case
still returns null, so the button still falls back to centerCity().

`fleetBounds` keeps its name despite no longer reading S.cars: it is
exported on window.DH_ACT1 and "the box the follow feature fits" is still
what it means.

Circle visibility, two changes that do different jobs:

  - Zone circles are now STROKED (color #E0453C, weight 1.6, opacity .55 at
    rest, previously `stroke:false`). The ring says "a zone is HERE" at a
    constant, always-legible weight regardless of the hour.
  - The fill range moved: divisor 18 -> 12, floor 0.02 -> 0.13, cap
    0.30 -> 0.50. A typical zone (base 5) at a typical curve (~1.0) now
    lands near 0.42 instead of near 0.28, and the quietest a live zone can
    look is 0.13 rather than a genuinely invisible 0.02.

The SHAPE of the shading is unchanged — base x hourly curve still orders the
zones exactly as before, so the map does not read differently, only more of
its range is actually perceptible. The stroke is faded in step with the fill
so a quiet zone reads quiet rather than outlined-but-empty, and both go to 0
when a zone is switched off, so the geofence still reads unambiguously.

TESTS

New test/recenter-zones.test.js: the label/aria-label/title and both
setFollow() titles are US-spelled and no player-facing "Recentre" survives;
fleetBounds() fits zones, skips geofenced-off ones, still pushes both
corners so an edge zone is not sliced, and contains neither CHARGERS nor
S.cars; it still returns null with nothing to frame (which is what the
centerCity() fallback depends on); zone circles are no longer stroke:false
and pass a colour and weight; the fill divisor dropped, the floor is >= 0.10,
the cap rose above 0.30, and floor is still below cap; the stroke fades with
the fill rather than staying at full weight.

One trap worth recording: the first version of that zone-loop assertion
regex matched buildCityLayers()'s one-line `zoneLayer.forEach(...removeLayer...)`
instead of drawMap()'s shading loop, and silently checked the wrong body. It
is now anchored on `const on=`.

Full suite passes clean, and check-parity is green — deploy/public/index.html
is byte-identical to deadhead.html.

0.47.3 — Shift stays tied to Clock

WHAT WAS WRONG

.tb-shiftitem carried margin-right:auto, which pinned the Shift readout to
the far-left edge of the topbar's second line while .tb-ctrls's own
justify-content:flex-end clustered everything else — Clock, the speed
segment, Garage/Saves/Night/Sound/profile/units — on the right. At most
widths that read as a wide dead gap between a label nobody presses and the
Clock button it's describing, rather than the two looking related.

WHAT CHANGED

Dropped the margin-right:auto. Shift is still the first child of .tb-ctrls
(unchanged HTML order), so it now sits in normal flow immediately before
Clock, and justify-content:flex-end clusters the whole line — Shift, Clock,
speeds, buttons — together against the right edge instead of splitting it
into a pinned label and a separate cluster.

TESTS

test/city.test.js's "the shift readout is pushed left, the controls stay
right" check asserted the OLD behaviour (.tb-shiftitem{margin-right:auto}
present). Replaced it with "the shift readout sits with the controls, not
pinned to the edge", asserting the rule is gone and justify-content:flex-end
still holds. The neighbouring check ("Shift leads the control line, not the
readouts" — Shift is .tb-ctrls's first child) needed no change: that part of
the layout didn't move, only the auto-margin did.

0.34.2 — Fleet roster's Buy button stays in view as the fleet grows

Pavel: with four cars in the roster, "Add a vehicle" sat below whatever
slice of the panel happened to be visible — reported as the button being
"below the view of the box".

WHAT CHANGED

#pb-fleet's metrics (Earning/Idle/Down), the vehicle table, and the Buy
button used to share one scrolling region — the whole panel body scrolled
as a single block, so growing the roster by a row pushed the button
further down that same scroll, with nothing marking it as reachable.

#pb-fleet is now a column: the metrics and the Buy button are fixed at
their natural size (flex:0 0 auto), and only the table sits inside its own
`.fleet-scroll` wrapper, which is the one thing allowed to scroll. Add a
5th car and the table gains a scrollbar; Buy stays exactly where it was.
Same pattern the Rapid network panel already uses for its charger list —
scoped to #pb-fleet specifically rather than changed on the shared
.p-body rule, since Platforms/Rapid/Books intentionally scroll as one
block and shouldn't inherit this.

Verified structurally via jsdom: #buy and .metrics are direct children of
#pb-fleet (outside the scroller), #fleet-rows lives inside .fleet-scroll,
and #buy follows the scroller in DOM order. Existing suite (8 files) and
check-parity still pass.

0.36.0 — Fleet/Energy/Books/Ops/Comms/Trophies show on the main display

Pavel: Trophies opened as a separate full-screen modal, and the wing
panels (Fleet roster, Rapid network, Books, Platforms, Messages) only
flashed in place when their nav icon was clicked — neither showed more
than the ~440-520px wing column ever had room for. He wanted the black
nav strip's icons to actually bring their content onto the console's own
screen, the same way "Take control" already does for Camera, so there's
room to see more rows than the side ever could.

WHAT CHANGED

A new #console-panel sits inside .map-wrap, layered over #map exactly the
way #dodge-incar already is — Leaflet is never hidden, resized, or torn
down; the overlay just covers it. Clicking Fleet/Energy/Books/Ops/Comms
now RELOCATES the real wing panel into it (via showInConsole()) rather
than flashing a highlight or cloning content: render() writes to these
ids wherever they live in the DOM, so moving the actual node is what
keeps everything staying in sync for free, with no second copy that
could drift. The wing slot the panel came from gets a quiet
"<name> is shown on the main display" placeholder card so the grid
doesn't collapse; switching straight from one panel to another restores
the first before showing the second, so at most one panel is ever
missing from the wing at a time.

Trophies moves the same way, just without a wing home to leave a
placeholder in — #trophy-card (its actual achievement grid + board,
pulled out of what used to be the full-screen #trophy modal) relocates
into the console instead, and #trophy itself stays permanently hidden/
empty from now on. openTrophies() is still the one entry point (called
from both the nav icon and the shift report's "Trophies · N new"
button), so neither caller needed to change.

Map closes whichever overlay is showing (dodge OR a console panel) and
returns to the map, same as before. Escape now closes an open
console-panel too, matching the dodge camera's existing Escape handling.
A small × in the corner (#cp-close) does the same thing for a mouse.

Verified with jsdom: clicking Fleet moves #panel-fleet into #cp-body and
drops a placeholder in the wing; clicking Energy right after restores
Fleet to the wing (placeholder gone) and shows Rapid network instead;
clicking Map restores whichever panel was showing and hides the overlay;
Trophies moves #trophy-card in and back out via the close button, with
#trophy itself staying hidden throughout; Escape closes an open panel.
Existing suite (8 files) and check-parity both still pass.

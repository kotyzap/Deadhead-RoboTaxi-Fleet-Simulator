0.51.0 — running a car flat now means something

WHAT WAS WRONG

Pavel asked what happens when a car hits 0% battery. The answer was nothing.

drive() floors state of charge with clamp(c.soc - kwh/sp.kwh*100, 0, 100), and
no code path anywhere tested for empty. So a car at 0% kept driving at full
speed, indefinitely, completing fares normally — and because energy is only
ever billed while plugged in (see the charging branch of stepCar), a
permanently-flat car ran for FREE: depreciation and insurance per mile, zero
energy, forever. The cheapest way to operate was to never charge.

What disguised this was CFG.reserveSoc (12), checked in assign(), the
queue-assign path and stepCar's queue drain: a car won't ACCEPT a ride it
can't finish with 12% spare. So normal play soft-locked instead — the car sat
idle refusing every offer while the fixed cost ran. But the reserve is only
consulted at assignment, so an incident re-route, or CFG.heatPenalty raising
consumption mid-trip past what was projected, still walked cars to zero.

Separately, and finally fixed: CFG.socAlertAmber was 25 against a chargeAt of
28. The Charge pill's amber band is `soc >= chargeAt && soc < socAlertAmber`,
i.e. 28 <= x < 25 — the empty set. The pill could only ever be green or red,
jumping between them with no warning state. Flagged twice during the 0.48.x
pill work and deliberately left alone both times pending a real number.

WHAT CHANGED

Two new car states. 'stranded': the car stops where it is, cannot move by any
route, and does NOT self-clear — that is the whole difference from 'blocked',
which resolves itself after c.needs seconds. Any fare in progress is abandoned
(counted as a cancellation, -2.5 safety, same as a rider walking), anything
queued behind it goes back to the open pool rather than waiting on a car that
will never come, and a pending charge order is voided. Both states are
crit-red, not attn-amber, and reuse the existing .d-crit/.t-crit classes.

'recovery': a flatbed, called from the console's charge button, which becomes
"Call flatbed" and names the price. CFG.recoveryFee ($180 — roughly a good
shift's net) is charged on the CALL, not on arrival, so the decision has an
immediate cost; it lands in S.d.cost so the shift report explains the night.
CFG.recoverySec (240) is real seconds, timed like the blocked window and the
offer clock so a 20x player doesn't get a 20x faster tow. The car is dropped
AT the nearest charger and plugged in to 100 (chManual), because nobody pays
for a flatbed to be topped up to 85%.

The strand check sits in stepCar's movement branch immediately after drive() —
the only call that can lower soc through movement — and BEFORE the incident
roll, since a car with nothing left doesn't also get to be blocked.

sendToCharge() refuses a stranded or recovering car. That guard is in the
function, not just the console handler, because the Rapid-charge panel's
per-site buttons call sendToCharge directly and its fall-through would have
set state='toCharge' and silently un-stranded the car for free.

nextTask() puts a flat car above everything, including "choose a vehicle" and
"clock on": it is the only state that cannot resolve itself and goes on owing
its fixed cost the entire time it sits there.

socAlertAmber is now 40, above chargeAt, so all three bands are reachable:
green above 40, amber 40 down to 28, red below 28. The pill's class expression
simplified accordingly (the redundant lower bound is gone).

SAVE: c.recovTo holds a live CHARGERS object, so it is stripped from the
snapshot and stored as recovToName, then re-pointed via chargerByName() on
restore — exactly the chIdx/chName trap, which JSON would otherwise turn into
a disjoint twin that no longer === anything in CHARGERS. A stranded car
reloads AS stranded (it is waiting on a decision, not mid-animation), unlike
the trip/charge states, which restore() already resets when their reference is
gone. No SAVE_V bump: the new fields are additive and absent fields default.

TESTS

New test/stranded.test.js, wired into npm test — 41 checks. The ones that
matter most: nothing makes a flat car move (stepCar, sendToCharge by nearest
AND by name, assign), it survives 200 steps without self-clearing, the fee is
charged exactly once on the call, the tow takes real seconds not sim seconds,
the queue is released, and recovTo round-trips by NAME with object identity
proved through chargerByName(). Plus a relationship assertion that
socAlertAmber stays above chargeAt, so retuning either number cannot silently
reintroduce the empty band.

Full suite passes clean (405 checks, up from 363).

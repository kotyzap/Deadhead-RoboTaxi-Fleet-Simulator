0.38.0 — a Recentre button, and the map follows the fleet on its own

Pavel: panning the map off left no way back short of hunting for the fleet by
hand, and a car that drove out of frame stayed out of frame. He asked for a
recentre control plus a periodic one every 30 seconds.

WHAT GOES IN THE BOX

Cars, active zones and chargers — the whole operation, Pavel's call, rather
than just the vehicles. Cars are road-snapped through roadFix() so the box
matches what drawMap() actually paints, not the straight-line model position
underneath it. Zone circles are 620 m radius, so both corners of each zone's
bounding square go in rather than its centre — a box fitted to centres slices
the edge zones in half. A geofenced-off zone drops out: it is not part of the
operation. Practical effect is that the fitted view is close to city-sized
most of the time and the periodic recentre is usually a no-op, which is the
intent — it is a safety net for a fleet that wandered or a player who panned
to Houston, not a camera that chases cars around.

AUTO-FOLLOW IS A TOGGLE, ON BY DEFAULT, AND PANNING TURNS IT OFF

This is the answer to the problem the city-switch code has flagged in a
comment since Dallas shipped: "centring again here would fight a player who
has already panned." The map may only take itself back while the player has
not taken the wheel. Dragging or zooming by hand is taking the wheel; the
button hands it back. So there is exactly one control for both jobs — press
it to recentre AND to resume following.

The button sits bottom-LEFT of the map (Leaflet's own zoom control is at
bottomright, attribution owns the rest of that edge), z-index 700: above
every marker and tooltip so it is always clickable, below #map-lock (1100) so
the mobile gesture shield still covers it. Following is the quiet resting
state; the accent fill appears only when the player has panned away and the
button has become the way back, which is the one moment it deserves an eye.

TWO THINGS THAT LOOK LIKE STYLE BUT ARE NOT

`animate:false` on the fit is load-bearing. fitBounds fires zoomstart — the
same event a player's own zoom fires — so recenterMap() has to hold a
followSuppress flag across it, and only a synchronous fit guarantees the flag
is still up when the event lands. An animated fit delivers zoomstart after
the flag is cleared and the map switches its own following off. Verified both
directions: a programmatic recentre leaves following ON, a real
dragstart/zoomstart switches it OFF.

The 30s timer is real seconds, matching the autosave interval, NOT sim
seconds — at 20x a 30-sim-second recentre would fire every 1.5 real seconds
and the map would twitch. Guards: only while following, only with a live map,
never while the tab is hidden. recenterMap() swallows its own errors (a
zero-size container makes Leaflet's zoom maths unhappy) so the interval can
never be thrown away.

initMap() now opens on the fitted view rather than a fixed z13, so
auto-follow's first tick has nothing to change and the player never sees an
unprompted jump 30 seconds in. loadCityTables() refits on a city switch for
the same reason — but only while following, so someone who has taken the
wheel keeps it across a switch too.

Also added: an `i-recenter` symbol to the sprite (corner brackets + centre
dot), since nothing in there read as "fit to view".

Verified with jsdom under a Leaflet stub (16 checks): initMap wires the
dragstart/zoomstart listener and fits once at boot with animate:false,
padding and maxZoom 15; the bounds contain exactly one point per car, two per
active zone and one per charger, with no NaN/Infinity and with an off zone
dropping out; a programmatic recentre keeps following, a real pan event drops
it, and the button restores it. Also verified the whole feature is inert and
throw-free with map===null, which is the real state whenever Leaflet fails to
load. Existing suite (8 files) and check-parity both pass.

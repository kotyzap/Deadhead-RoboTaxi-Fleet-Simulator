0.50.1 — the shift report fits the sheet again, and Margin stopped being "argin"

WHAT WAS WRONG

Three things, all visible in one screenshot of 0.50.0:

1. The report scrolled. Adding the hexagraph to a report that was already
   fifteen rows pushed it past .rep's max-height:86vh, so the title scrolled
   off the top and the chart was cut by the sheet's edge.

2. The left-hand axis labels were clipped. They are drawn with
   text-anchor="end", so their text extends LEFTWARD from an anchor at about
   x=0.14*size — which in a square viewBox starting at 0 runs off the edge.
   Nothing inside an SVG can paint outside its viewBox, so "Margin" rendered
   as "argin". Same class of bug as the charge ring in 0.48.1: correct
   geometry, wrong container, and it fails silently rather than loudly.

3. "Shift length" and "Hours worked" were the SAME NUMBER printed twice under
   two names — both S.workedSec/3600, eleven rows apart. Pavel's screenshot
   showed 1.3 h in both.

WHAT CHANGED

Layout: the chart and a new right-hand column are siblings in .rp-top.
Distance driven, Operating cost per unit and Platform take rate move out of
the ledger into that column as .rp-mini blocks (label above value — a narrow
column has no room for .rp-row's space-between). Pavel's call, and it earns
its keep twice: it reclaims three rows AND fills the ~140px of dead space
that a centred 236px chart left on either side of a 520px sheet. The axis
legend and the ghost key move into the same column. Below 470px the two
columns stack and the readouts run as a row underneath.

"Shift length" is deleted, not moved. "Hours worked" is the one that stays,
because it is half of the worked-vs-billed pair the whole act is built to
deliver, and that pair only reads as a pair when the two lines sit together.
The hours-billed line also loses its trailing sentence ("the car owes X a day
whether you work or not" -> "X/day regardless") — it wrapped onto a second
line inside a 13px row and cost more height than it earned. The full claim
survives in Paolo's beat 13, where there is room for it.

The viewBox is now padded horizontally (PAD_X 26, PAD_Y 8) instead of square,
and the hexagon is NOT shrunk to make room — the box carries the label gutter
itself. PAD_Y is smaller because the top and bottom labels are centred rather
than anchored outward, so they only need their own ascender/descender.

Also fixed while in here: rpRadarBlock() returned '' with no history row,
which after this change would have silently taken the three per-distance
readouts down with the chart. They now render as their own .rp-nochart row.

Measured after: ~497px of body against ~654px available at a 900px viewport
(86vh less title and buttons). Eleven .rp-row plus three .rp-mini, down from
fifteen rows.

TESTS

shift-radar.test.js grows to 43 checks. The label-gutter ones are the
interesting addition: they parse the emitted viewBox and every
text-anchor="end" element out of the real SVG string and assert each anchor
has ~34px of room inside the left edge — so the gutter cannot be quietly
removed by someone tidying the viewBox back to "0 0 n n", and the check
re-derives itself if the label radius is ever retuned. Plus: the chart and
side column are siblings, the three readouts are in the column and NOT also
full-width rows, "Shift length" stays gone, "Hours worked" stays, the wrapped
sentence stays gone, and the chartless path keeps its readouts.

jsdom does no layout, so the height itself is not assertable here — what is
asserted is the structure that bought the height back. Full suite passes
clean (363 checks, up from 351).

0.53.0 — Coffee joins the gear, and a whole layout mechanism retires with it

WHAT WAS WRONG

Nothing was broken — Pavel's call: "I should not be that greedy." The donation
button had been pinned top-right in the topbar since v0.26.3, and keeping it
there cost more layout machinery than any other single element in the file:

  - #coffee{order:2;margin-left:auto;align-self:flex-end} for the narrow
    arrangement, plus an `order` swap at >=2250px for the wide one.
  - --coffee-w, a custom property whose only job was reserving horizontal room
    so .tb-info could never claim the full width and bump the button onto a line
    of its own. It did exactly that at 2115px, and again at ~885px after the
    first fix. The value had been retuned three times (116/56/112px, then
    collapsed to one 48px in 0.51.1 when the button went icon-only).
  - .tb-info{max-width:calc(100% - var(--coffee-w))} and a >=2250px override
    releasing that cap again.

WHAT CHANGED

Coffee is the fourth row of #settings-pop, under a real gap rather than another
hairline (.set-give) — it is an ASK, not a setting, and shouldn't read as one
more toggle. It keeps its id, so rayCoffee() still binds to it untouched.

All of the machinery above is DELETED, not orphaned: no rule targets #coffee,
--coffee-w is neither declared nor read anywhere, and .tb-info has no max-width.
The topbar is down to two children (.tb-info, .tb-ctrls) and the >=2250px tier
now does one thing — un-force the controls' full-width break. This is the
simplest that row has been since it gained a wrap.

TWO THINGS THAT WOULD HAVE BROKEN QUIETLY

rayAtCoffee() places Paolo's coffee card by measuring a button to sit under, and
it measured #coffee. Coffee is inside the popover now, and its click handler
closes that popover before opening the card — so it would have measured an
element inside a display:none subtree, where getBoundingClientRect() returns all
zeros, and the card would have pinned itself to the top-left corner of the
screen. It measures #settings-btn instead: the gear is visible, is where the
player just came from, and is where the popover was hanging off anyway.

The Coffee click handler also has to close the menu explicitly, because
#settings-pop is z-index 1320 — above #ray — so the menu would otherwise cover
the card it just opened.

TESTS

Four city.test.js checks encoded the old pinning and were rewritten:

  - the topbar's child count is two, not three.
  - a new "the Coffee-pinning layout mechanism is fully removed" asserts the
    ABSENCE of #coffee{...}, of any --coffee-w declaration, and of any
    var(--coffee-w) read — while confirming .tb-ctrls still takes its own line
    and the wide tier still releases it. Asserting absence matters here: a dead
    layout rule that still matches a live selector is exactly how
    .topbar .ghost.units went stale in 0.52.0.
  - .tb-info carries no max-width.
  - the icon-only check covers the gear alone now, Coffee having gained a label.

Two new behavioural checks: rayAtCoffee()'s source measures #settings-btn, and
clicking Coffee closes the menu while opening Paolo's card. jsdom has no layout,
so the resulting card POSITION is not assertable — which is precisely why the
anchor target is asserted in the source instead.

Full suite passes clean (405 checks).

0.26.7 — one message style for Paolo, and the bubble is a block

The locked-city reply was squeezed and hard to read, and the cause was a real
bug rather than taste: `.bubble` had no `display`, so it stayed inline, and a
multi-line inline box paints its background and padding per LINE FRAGMENT instead
of around the paragraph. Text spilled past the grey and the avatar drifted to the
floor.

- display:block on the bubble and the column, which is what makes a paragraph
  paint as one rounded box. The builder emits divs, not spans.
- THE BUBBLE RULES NOW LIVE IN ONE PLACE. #ray and #lockmsg list themselves as
  selectors on the same declarations — avatar, name line, grey, radius, night
  variant — so Paolo looks like Paolo whether he is speaking from the card or
  answering a locked city, in every city, in both themes. A third surface should
  add its selector, never copy the values.
- The requirement is now a SECOND BUBBLE rather than a tinted caption. Two
  messages in a row is how a person texts a rule after an opinion, and it stops
  the sentence that matters most being set in the smallest, palest type on
  screen — which is what the city-tinted caption was.
- Width is min(330px, 100vw - 24px) with slightly more padding, so a long line
  has room and a narrow window still fits.

Two checks: the reply is built from block elements in two bubbles, and the shared
selectors exist so the two surfaces cannot drift. Mutation-tested by removing
display:block.

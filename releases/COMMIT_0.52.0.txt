0.52.0 — one gear instead of three buttons

WHAT WAS WRONG

Theme, sound and units were three separate topbar controls taking three
button-widths of a row that has been fighting for space since v0.26.3 (see the
wrap history in the .topbar comment). None of the three is something you touch
during a shift — they are settings, and settings belong behind one affordance.

WHAT CHANGED

A gear button (#settings-btn, new #i-gear symbol) opens #settings-pop, a small
popover with three labelled rows: Theme, Sound, Distance. Net saving in the
topbar is two controls; the row now ends with Garage, Saves, gear, Coffee.

THE THREE CONTROLS KEPT THEIR IDS. #theme, #sound and #units are the same
elements they always were, moved into the popover — so the theme toggle,
syncSoundBtn() and paintUnits() all still bind to exactly what they bound to
before, and none of that code needed touching beyond two added lines that
write the new row labels. Rebuilding them as fresh elements would have left
three controls that look right and do nothing.

WHERE IT LIVES, AND WHY IT MATTERS. #settings-pop is a sibling of .app, not a
child of .topbar or .app, for two independent reasons — both of which fail
silently:

  - .topbar carries backdrop-filter, and a backdrop-filter ancestor becomes the
    containing block for position:fixed descendants. Nested in the topbar, the
    popover would be positioned against the TOPBAR rather than the viewport,
    and clipped by its rounded corners.
  - .app is position:relative z-index:2 — a stacking context nothing inside can
    escape. This is the same reasoning already recorded in the
    #dh-console-panel note for why the modals sit outside .app.

Because it is not inside the button it belongs to, it is placed from the gear's
own getBoundingClientRect() by setPopPlace(): right-aligned to the gear,
clamped to the viewport so it never hangs off a narrow window, and flipped
above the button when there is no room below (the gear can land on the lower of
the topbar's two lines). offsetWidth is zero while hidden, so placement runs
AFTER the unhide, not before. A resize re-anchors rather than closes, since the
topbar rewraps at several widths and the gear moves with it.

Closes on: outside click, Escape (returning focus to the gear), or a second
click on the gear. The gear's own click calls stopPropagation — without it the
click bubbles to the document close-listener and the menu opens and shuts in
the same gesture, which looks completely dead. Clicks inside the popover stop
propagating too, so flipping the theme and then the units does not require
reopening the menu between them.

Two rules relocated rather than left dangling: .topbar .ghost.units (tabular
numerals, so mi<->km does not reflow its row) is now #settings-pop .ghost.units
— scoped to .topbar it would have matched nothing. And the sub-1500px
label-hiding rule gained a comment noting that #settings-pop is out of its
reach by construction, so the popover rows keep their labels at every width.

TESTS

city.test.js: the 0.51.1 icon-only check now covers the gear and Coffee (the
two icon-only topbar buttons left). Nine new checks — the popover is outside
both .topbar and .app, the three controls kept their ids and are inside it,
none is left in the topbar, it starts closed with correct aria-expanded/
aria-controls, it is position:fixed above the modals with a working [hidden]
rule, the units tabular sizing followed it, and four behavioural ones covering
open, use-without-closing, outside-click, Escape and re-click. The
"use without closing" check is the one that matters most: it is the
stopPropagation regression, which is invisible in CSS and total in effect.

Full suite passes clean (405 checks).

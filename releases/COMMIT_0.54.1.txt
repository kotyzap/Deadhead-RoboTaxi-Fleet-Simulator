0.54.1 — Day/Clock/Cash move to the control line, so the topbar stops being three lines deep

WHAT WAS WRONG

Pavel's screenshot at ~1024px: the topbar was rendering three lines —
brand+tabs+Day+Clock on the first, Cash alone on a second, Shift+Clock on+
speeds+icons on a third. Day/Clock/Cash lived inside .tb-info alongside the
brand and city-tab strip, and .tb-info wraps internally — so when the block
was JUST short of room, the wrap landed wherever the arithmetic put it: Cash,
alone, while Day and Clock stayed wedged next to the tabs one line up. An
earlier pass the same day (0.54.0's predecessor work) tightened .tb-info's
column gaps to buy back width, which helped but did not fix the class of bug —
gaps are a width budget, not a grouping rule, and nothing told the layout that
Day/Clock/Cash belong together as one unit.

WHAT CHANGED

Day, Clock and Cash moved out of .tb-info and into .tb-ctrls, ahead of Shift.
.tb-ctrls only ever moves as one atomic block — that has been true since
0.53.1, when the row was cut down to exactly two children so the only seam a
natural wrap can pick is the one between them. Putting the three readouts in
that block means they now travel WITH Shift/Clock on/speeds/icons rather than
wrapping loose inside .tb-info: at any width, the seam is either "brand+tabs
alone" or "brand+tabs, PLUS every readout and every control together" — never
a stray middle line.

.tb-info is left holding just the brand, the city-tab strip, and the Trophies
readout (already the first thing hidden at narrower widths via .tb-opt).
Shift still sits immediately before the Clock button it describes — that
adjacency didn't change, only the fact that it's no longer literally the
first child of .tb-ctrls; Day is.

TESTS

One test hard-asserted the old rule ("Shift leads the control line") via
`ctrls.firstElementChild === shift`, which is now false by design. Replaced
with a check for the actual invariant that matters: Day, Clock, Cash and
Shift are all inside .tb-ctrls (none leaked back into .tb-info), in that
order, and Shift still sits immediately before the #clock button. Updated two
stale comments (the gap-tightening rule, the T4b tier's "read line / press
line" description) that referenced the old grouping.

Verified headlessly at 1024px CSS width (Playwright + local Leaflet, same
recipe as 0.54.0): two lines, brand+tabs on the first, Day/Clock/Cash/Shift/
Clock on/speeds/icons together on the second, no orphaned Cash.

Full suite passes clean (`npm test`, 405 checks), and `check-parity` is
green — deploy/public/index.html is byte-identical to deadhead.html.

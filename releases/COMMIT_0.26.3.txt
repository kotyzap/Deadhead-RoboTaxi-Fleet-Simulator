0.26.3 — the topbar breaks in one chosen place, and Coffee stays top-right

At 2115px the row wrapped and put the Coffee button alone on a line of its own,
which reads as a mistake rather than a layout.

Cause: the topbar was a flat flex list of seventeen items, so once wrapping was
switched on in v0.26.1 the seam landed wherever the arithmetic put it. Wrapping
was the right fix for content running off the right edge; leaving the row flat
was not.

The row now has exactly THREE children, so there is only one seam it can come
apart at:

  narrow:  [ readouts .......................... Coffee ]
           [ .................. Clock · speeds · buttons ]
  wide:    [ readouts .... Clock · speeds · buttons · Coffee ]

- .tb-info holds the brand, the city cards and the six readouts, and grows so
  Coffee is pushed to the right edge.
- .tb-ctrls holds everything from Clock on rightwards and takes a full line of
  its own, right-aligned so it sits under Coffee rather than drifting left.
- #coffee is pinned to the top-right corner in both arrangements. It is a
  donation button: findable, never a stray.

The two arrangements are the same three items with `order` swapped, so there is
no duplicate markup. The single-line case is now the exception, behind
@media (min-width:2250px) — measured rather than guessed: brand + five city
cards + six readouts + Clock + four speeds + five buttons + Coffee and their gaps
come to roughly 2200px, which is exactly why a 2115px window wrapped.

The empty .spacer div is gone. .tb-info's flex-grow does the same job, and a
flex:1 spacer in a wrapping row claims a whole line to itself — the same class of
bug as the one being fixed here.

Three checks, one structural (the row must have three children with Coffee last)
and two on the order swap and the dead spacer. Mutation-tested.

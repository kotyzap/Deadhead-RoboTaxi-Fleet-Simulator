0.66.0 — smarter busy-car queueing, and a free bail-out on a snap decline

Pavel: opened a design discussion (2026-07-30) about letting the player
assign cars to offers directly — "the target of the game" per his framing.
Landed as two smaller, automatic changes instead of a manual picker, agreed
in that discussion: assign()'s busy-car scoring now measures deadhead from
where a car's queue actually ENDS rather than where the car IS right now,
and declining an offer within a few seconds of it appearing no longer costs
the platform relationship. A manual car-picker UI remains a possible next
step, not built here.


WHAT CHANGED

  1. assign()'s busy-car branch (deadhead.html / deploy/public/index.html,
     identical forks) used to break ties with remainKm(c) — distance from
     the car's LIVE position to the end of its current leg. That rewards a
     car for merely being nearby right now, even if it still has that whole
     leg (and anything queued behind it) to drive first. New helper
     queueEndPoint(c) returns the last queued ride's dropoff, or the current
     route's end if the queue is empty, and the score is now
     queue.length*1000 + distance(queueEndPoint(c), ride.pickup). Queue depth
     still dominates the score by the same x1000 margin, so an empty car
     never loses to a well-placed but already-backed-up one.

  2. CFG.declineGraceSec (3) is a new config value, well under the 4s floor
     of offerDecideBySpeed at 20x. Offers now carry leftInit alongside left
     (spawnRides(), guideForceOffer()) — a copy of the starting countdown
     that is never decremented, so leftInit-left is elapsed real seconds
     since the offer appeared regardless of any speed change mid-countdown.
     declineOffer() walks p.offered back down by one when a player decline
     (not a silent timeout) lands inside that window, since p.offered was
     already bumped at spawn time, before the player had a chance to look.
     A slower decline, or any unattended timeout, costs exactly what it did
     before.

  3. deploy/test/smart-dispatch.test.js (new) covers both: queueEndPoint
     tracking route-end over live position, queue depth still outweighing
     proximity, a snap decline being walked back out of acceptRate's
     denominator, a considered decline still costing it, and a silent
     timeout being untouched by the grace logic. Wired into package.json's
     test script. Both functions also gained entries in the DH_ACT1 export
     block (queueEndPoint, spawnRides) so the test can exercise them
     directly instead of re-deriving offer/queue shapes by hand.


NOT BUILT HERE, ON PURPOSE

  No player-facing car picker. Pavel's stated goal is eventually letting the
  player choose which car takes an accepted offer, but this pass is scoped
  to making the existing automatic assignment less naive first, per his own
  call not to rebuild the mechanic. Idle-car selection (nearest-only) is
  also untouched — only the busy-car queueing path changed.


TESTS

  Full suite run from deploy/: all green, including the new
  smart-dispatch.test.js and the pre-existing version.test.js (which
  requires a COMMIT_0.66.0.txt matching const VERSION — this file).

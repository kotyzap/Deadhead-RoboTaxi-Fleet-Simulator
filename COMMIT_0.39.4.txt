0.39.4 — Phoenix and Las Vegas, as a mockup only

Pavel, asked about future cities: "how about just making the tab in top bar with the lock and
when clicked Paolo will say something... No development, just graphic mockup button."

WHAT THIS IS NOT

Not a scenario. No CITIES entry, no ZONES_BY_CITY, no CHARGERS_BY_CITY, no economy, no gate,
no save data. Phoenix and Las Vegas do not exist anywhere gateMet()/cityUnlocked()/cityList()
can see them, on purpose — those all assume a real, playable city behind the lock, and there
isn't one yet. If either becomes real later, it goes through the same process San Francisco
just did (research the real launch, add it to CITIES properly) and drops out of this list
entirely.

WHAT IT IS

Two permanently-locked cards appended to the end of the city tab strip after the six real
ones, rendered from a new PLANNED_CITIES array kept deliberately separate from CITIES. Clicking
one reuses the existing #lockmsg element and positioning logic (see cityLockMsg) but shows a
single Paolo line — no second "requirement" bubble, because there is no gate to name:

  Phoenix: "Tesla's talked about it out loud, but nobody's signed anything I've seen. Keep
  clearing shifts here — you'll be first through that door whenever it's actually a door."

  Las Vegas: "A rumor with good odds, way I hear it. Nothing's running yet. Clock your shifts,
  and I'll be the one who tells you the day that stops being true."

Both decline to promise a date, which is the honest thing to say about either city right now.

HOW IT STAYS OUT OF THE WAY

renderCityTabs() appends the two mockup buttons after the real cityList() ones, keyed
data-planned="phx"/"lv" instead of data-city, with no tone colour at all (there is no
scenario's colour to show) rather than inheriting the live city's tint. The existing city-tab
click handler gets one new branch ahead of its data-city check, so a click on a planned card
never reaches cityUnlocked()/switchCity() and can't accidentally "unlock" into a city that
doesn't exist. Every existing city-tab test that iterates ".citytab" was narrowed to
".citytab[data-city]" so the six real-city checks (tone colours, night-mode repaint, "every
city gets a card") don't see the two mockup cards and misread them as CITIES entries missing a
tone. Three new checks cover the mockup itself: both cards render locked with no data-city,
neither id exists in cityList()/CITIES, and clicking one shows Paolo's line without touching
S.city.

Existing suite (8 files) and check-parity pass.

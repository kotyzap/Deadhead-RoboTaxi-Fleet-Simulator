0.64.4 — City picker: the name never gets dropped or squeezed to nothing

WHAT WAS WRONG

Two follow-ups from 0.64.3's city dropdown, both reported by Pavel on
2026-07-30 against real screenshots:

  1. "Always display the city name, do not compress it to 'dot' only."
     A pre-existing rule (`.topbar .ghost:not(.units)>span{display:none}`,
     written for Garage/Saves at the 1499px tier) matched #city-pick-label's
     span too, since .citypick shares the plain .ghost class. Below 1499px
     — which the specificity note beside it explains is actually EVERY
     width down to phone, not just the tablet tier the comment claims —
     the picker collapsed to just the tone dot and a chevron with no city
     name at all.
  2. At a laptop-width screenshot (inside the same 1499px tier), the dot
     was touching "Austin" with no space between them. `.topbar .ghost` at
     that tier sets `gap:0` for every ghost button — right for Garage/
     Saves/Gear, which are icon-only there and have nothing to space out,
     but it also zeroed .citypick's own gap between its three children.

WHAT CHANGED

(1) `.topbar .ghost:not(.units)>span{display:none}` gained a
`:not(.citypick)`, so the label survives regardless of width.

(2) Added `.topbar .citypick{gap:6px}` right after the zeroing rule — one
class more specific than `.topbar .ghost`, so it wins regardless of source
order, restoring the dot/name/chevron spacing at the exact tier the
screenshot was taken in.

TESTS

No test changes needed — city.test.js's existing
"#city-pick shows the current city's short name and tone colour" check
already asserts the label's textContent, which was passing because jsdom
applies no stylesheet at all (a display:none span still has non-empty
textContent) — this was a real-browser-only bug that a DOM-content
assertion can't see. Full suite re-run clean (30 test files); check-parity
green.

CAR ROTATION

carHeading() returns a compass bearing wrapped to 0-360, and that raw number
was written straight onto .cp-rot's transform every frame. .cp-rot has
`transition:transform .25s linear` so the turn animates — but a CSS
transition animates the literal number it is given, not the shortest way
around a circle. Going from rotate(350deg) to rotate(10deg) is a real 20
degree turn, but the transition doesn't know 350 and 10 are 20 degrees
apart the short way; it interpolates 350 down to 10 and the car visibly
spins backward through 340 degrees to get there. That is the "270 instead
of 90" report exactly: a genuine ~90 degree turn that happened to straddle
the 0/360 seam rendered as a ~270 degree turn instead, and a compass bearing
crosses that seam constantly — Doral is nearly due north of the pickup in
the screenshot, so this fired on ordinary road-following, not on some edge
case.

Fixed by keeping an UNWRAPPED, continuous angle per marker (m._hdg, stored
on the Leaflet marker object, not on the car — it is view state, not sim
state, and never touches snapshot()/restore()). Each frame, drawMap() steps
that value toward the new bearing by the signed delta ((target-cur+540)%360
-180), which is always the shortest turn, then writes the running total
(which can go past 360 or below 0) to the transform. The CSS transition now
only ever animates the real angular distance, in either direction.

OFFER ORDERING

$('offer-list') rendered S.offers in push order — oldest first — so a fresh
offer with a live countdown landed at the BOTTOM of the list, under
whatever had already arrived that shift. Combined with #waiting-list
(already-accepted rides still waiting for a free car, which has no cap and
only grows) sitting ABOVE #offer-list in the markup, an actionable "Accept /
Decline, 37s" offer could end up under two or three waiting entries — which
is exactly Pavel's screenshot.

Two changes:
  - #offer-list and #waiting-list swapped in the markup, so the list that
    demands a decision sits above the list that is just "still pending,
    nothing to do."
  - Both lists now render newest-first. offer-list takes S.offers.slice(
    -CFG.offerCap) (the most recent offerCap entries, in case more exist
    than the cap shows) and reverses it; waiting-list reverses its filtered
    array the same way. Neither array's actual order changes — S.offers is
    still pushed to and read from CFG.offerCap-first elsewhere (offerNet
    sort, the best-offer CTA, etc.) — this only touches what render() prints
    into the two lists.

Verified with jsdom (7 checks): the unwrap step function takes +20 (not
-340) across the 350->10 seam and the reverse -20 across 10->350, a clean
0->90 turn stays 90, and a repeated target produces zero delta; three
offers pushed in order A, B, C render as C, B, A in #offer-list; #offer-list
precedes #waiting-list in document order. Existing suite (8 files) and
check-parity pass.

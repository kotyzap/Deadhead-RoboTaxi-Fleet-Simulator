0.49.0 — Paolo warns before you clock off with the apps still connected

WHAT WAS WRONG

clockOff() wipes S.offers, every car's queue, and S.rides outright the
instant the shift ends — any accepted-but-unfinished ride, and the fare with
it, is just gone. Disconnecting a platform only stops NEW offers from
arriving; it does nothing to rides already on the board. There was no signal
telling the player that, so clocking off with a live feed or a backlog
quietly threw money away with no way to know it in advance.

WHAT CHANGED

Clicking Clock off while connected to a live platform now shows a Paolo card
before anything is touched — "Sign off the apps first," naming the connected
platform(s) by name and quoting the real dollar amount at risk (S.rides
summed through offerNet(), the same function the CTA and Decline button
already trust — never a hardcoded number). Two ways out: "Got it" just
closes the card, nothing happens; "Clock off anyway" is a real action — it
calls clockOff() directly.

The trigger is livePlatforms().length, not S.rides.length: a connected
fleet with an empty board can still have a ride land the instant you click,
so it warns even then, worded for "whatever lands gets thrown away" instead
of naming a ride count of zero.

This intercepts the Clock button's own click handler rather than living
inside clockOff() itself — clockOff(true), the bankruptcy/game-over path, is
not a voluntary click and must never hand out advice on the way to a game
over screen. Confirmed it doesn't: a live platform + pending ride, run
through clockOff(true) directly, shows no card.

'clockoff' joins 'advice'/'coffee'/'dodge'/'cityunlock' as one of Paolo's
string-tagged ad-hoc cards, not a scripted beat number — rayDismiss()'s
typeof guard already knew to leave those alone. "Clock off anyway" was
routed to avoid raySkip(): that call permanently sets S.ray.skipped and
silences every future tutorial beat and piece of advice, and choosing to
proceed here despite the warning is not the same gesture as telling Paolo to
go away for good. Skips entirely — Clock off just proceeds — if the player
already has S.ray.skipped set, or a scripted beat is already on screen; in
both cases this feature has nothing of its own to interrupt.

TESTS

New test/clockoff-warn.test.js, added to the npm test script. Covers: no
card when unconnected; the card fires even with an empty board while
connected; the copy names the platform and quotes the true at-risk total via
offerNet/money; "Got it" dismisses without clocking off or touching
S.ray.skipped; "Clock off anyway" really clocks off and does NOT set
S.ray.skipped; S.ray.skipped and an active scripted beat both bypass the
card; clockOff(true) (bankruptcy) never shows it. The fixture marks every
scripted beat seen and every city pre-unlocked, because clockOff() runs
progGates() and startFastForward() synchronously — without that, an
unrelated "Dallas unlocked" or next-day "connect a platform" advice card
would fire during the same click and get mistaken for this feature's own.

Full suite passes clean (317 checks), including the pre-existing company-
bank real-clock check this run. This release only touches the Clock
button's click handler, rayDismiss()'s ray-skip branch, restore()'s ray-cur
reset list, and the DH_ACT1 export — nothing in the billing/date path.

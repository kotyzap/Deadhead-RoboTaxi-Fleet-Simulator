0.46.2 — the way out pulses, once

A full-screen modal, nothing left that MUST be done, and the only move is a
small button in the top-right corner that was deliberately styled to be quiet.
For a first-time player that is a dead end: the cards all still look clickable
and nothing says the garage is finished with you.

#gar-close now takes `.call` in addition to `.go` when BOTH:

  - a car is in service (an empty fleet still faces a forced decision, and
    pulsing the exit then would be telling the player to leave with no car), and
  - S.shiftNo === 0 — no shift has ever been clocked on, i.e. this is the first
    visit of a new run. From the second visit on it is a plain accent outline.

Unlike .avail's three-cycle pulse (0.45.1) this one does not stop, and the
difference is deliberate: .avail marks several buttons across nine cards and an
endless animation there is a fairground, whereas this is exactly one element,
it is wayfinding rather than emphasis, and it ends the moment it is clicked.

prefers-reduced-motion needs its own rule again — the global animation:none
would otherwise leave a flat filled button with no draw at all, so the ring
becomes a held state instead of a pulse.

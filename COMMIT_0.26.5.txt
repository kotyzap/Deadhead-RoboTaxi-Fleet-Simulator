0.26.5 — Coffee rides with the readouts instead of taking a line

Same complaint as 0.26.3, one wrap tier further in. At ~885px the readout block
wrapped to three lines, filled 100% of the row, and pushed Coffee onto a fourth
line by itself.

Grouping alone could not fix that: .tb-info is a single flex item, so if its
content fills the width there is nothing left for its sibling. So the room is now
reserved rather than hoped for — .tb-info is capped at
calc(100% - var(--coffee-w)), and --coffee-w tracks how wide the button actually
is at that tier: 116px with a label, 56px where the topbar reduces it to an icon
(<=1499px), 112px again below 1200px where the labels come back, and no cap at all
above 2250px where the whole row is one line.

align-self:flex-end puts it level with the LAST line of the readout block — next
to Shift when the block has wrapped, next to Cash when it has not. On a
single-line topbar the flex line is exactly one control tall, so that is
identical to centring: correct at every width, aligned where it matters.

Two checks, one for the order swap and one that the reservation exists at every
tier. Mutation-tested by removing the cap.

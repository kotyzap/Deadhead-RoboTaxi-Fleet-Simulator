0.25.0 — Tampa and Orlando: five cities, one chain

Cities #4 and #5, both launched for real on 21 July 2026. The chain is now
Austin → Dallas → Miami → Tampa → Orlando, each gated on one shift clocked off
in the city before it, and a test asserts the chain itself rather than the
individual gates: every city's `needs` must name its immediate predecessor.

DESIGN.md §5 had these two down as the "tourism" scenario — airport and
theme-park demand, strong seasonality — and that was wrong for the third time
running. Orlando's real geofence is a corridor along Semoran Blvd and Lee Vista
Blvd that runs PAST MCO without including it, with Disney 20+ km away. Tampa's
is downtown, Ybor City, Tampa Heights, West Tampa and Hyde Park, excluding TPA
and every suburb. No new demand curve was needed; three of five shipped cities
now have no airport run, because that is what Tesla's early geofences look like.

TAMPA is the mirror of Miami — all core, no suburbs — and the inversion is
measured, not thematic. Evening/morning offer ratio: Miami 0.59, Orlando 0.81,
Tampa 1.42. Tampa's 21:00 is 25.1/h against 17.7/h at the 06:00 open, and 46.0/h
with the three Ybor taprooms live, the largest single hour in the game. Charging
inverts too: both in-geofence Superchargers are 150 kW, both 250 kW sites are out
of town, so the trade is "charge slowly in the box or leave the evening you came
for". fareK 0.92, the lowest of the five — a small dense core means the shortest
trips in the game. TECO off-peak 7.5c/kWh is the cheapest night power.

ORLANDO is the thinnest city in the game, next to an airport it cannot serve:
18.2/h at open, 14.8/h at 21:00, 31.5/h ceiling with the whole corridor live —
half of Tampa's. A big fleet starves, hence cap 14 and the lowest goal. It is
also the first city with NO brewery zone: Orlando's beer scene is 8–14 km outside
the box, and inventing service area is the one thing the city tables don't do.
The number that shows it — opening every zone buys +74% to +155% of evening
demand everywhere else and +0% here. Compensations are real: OUC is municipal
with the cheapest power of any scenario, and Lee Vista Blvd is 8 stalls at
325 kW, the fastest hardware and the only site inside the geofence, so the
charger queue penalty finally matters.

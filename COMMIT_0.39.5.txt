WHAT SUPERVISED ACTUALLY MEANS HERE

Every other city's permit field is reportage — Austin is ALSO permit:'Supervised' and has never
needed a staffing mechanic for it. SF is the one scenario where "a safety driver rides along"
is read literally: the driver is the player, not an employee. That reading drives a new,
dedicated CITIES.sf.soloSeat flag (never inferred from permit, which stays reportage everywhere
else) and three mechanics fall out of it together:

  1. fleetCap: 12 -> 1. Not "SF's fleet is small" — SF has no fleet, it has one car, because it
     has one of you. You cannot be in two seats at once.
  2. Simulated time cannot outrun real time. setSpeed() clamps to 1x for any soloSeat city
     REGARDLESS OF CALLER — the two speed-control input handlers, clockOn's CFG.defaultSpeed,
     closeDodge restoring the pre-mini-game speed, the pause-resume button all inherit the cap
     for free rather than each needing to know soloSeat exists. The controls themselves go
     visibly disabled (paintSoloSeat(), same `.cell[title]+button[disabled]` convention as
     #cpo-up/#cpo-down) so the lock is legible, not just silently enforced.
  3. CFG.soloStipend ($250) is credited at billMidnight() on any CALENDAR day you actually
     clocked on — a wage for the job, not a subsidy for owning the car, silent on a day you
     never worked. S.workedToday is the new calendar-day-scoped flag that makes that distinction
     (separate from S.workedSec, which is shift-scoped and reset by clockOn()).

A NEW ONE-TIME PAOLO LINE, not per-run: PROG.soloSeatTold (alongside PROG.achvTold, same
reasoning) fires once, ever, the first time a soloSeat city opens, and never repeats even
across a bankruptcy/restart of that same city.

A REAL BUG THIS SURFACED AND FIXED, unrelated to SF specifically: progTrack() — which already
runs every render() frame while a city is active — never wrote PROG.companyCash. Every OTHER
S.cash change (fares, energy, rent, and now the stipend) only ever touched S.cash, so
switchCity()'s autosave could park a city's LATEST earnings nowhere, and the next
newFleet({keepCompanyCash:true}) would open the next city on a stale balance. progTrack() now
mirrors S.cash into PROG.companyCash every frame (cheap: one number, no I/O — progSave() still
persists it on its own separate schedule). Without this fix the SF stipend itself would have
been the first thing to visibly evaporate on a city switch.

ALSO FIXED: affordWhy()'s fleet-cap message said "capped at 1 cars" for any city whose cap is 1
— SF was about to be the only city that could ever say a sentence that ungrammatical out loud.

test/soloseat.test.js (new, wired into npm test): fleetCap/soloSeat config, the grammar fix, the
speed clamp from every angle (setSpeed() directly, the disabled controls after render(), the
click handler honoring the disabled state, leaving SF lifting the lock), the stipend (paid only
when worked, silent elsewhere, never paid outside SF), workedToday's save/restore round trip
(including the "save from before this field existed" default), the one-time Paolo line, and the
progTrack()/companyCash fix.

Existing suite (10 files) and check-parity pass.

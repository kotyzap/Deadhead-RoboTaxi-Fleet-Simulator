0.39.3 — San Francisco, city #6, and the Act 2 caveat was wrong

Pavel, on being told SF was "blocked on Act 2": "I was not originally thinking about some
'hire other operator'... can we assume self-driving cars will be driving in SF, can we add it
now as another city?"

THE MISTAKE, CAUGHT

DESIGN.md and citiesplan.md both filed SF as blocked on Act 2, reasoning that its mandatory
safety monitor IS Act 2's payroll mechanic. That was wrong. `permit` has never been a payroll
system anywhere in this codebase — it's a label (`S.permit`) plus a couple of economy
multipliers (`insK`, the goal text). Austin has been `permit:'Supervised'` since v0.23.2 and
has never needed a hired-operator system for it. SF ships the exact same way.

WHAT'S REAL

Researched at ship time, not assumed:
- Tesla's Bay Area service runs FSD (Supervised) with an actual safety driver in the seat — the
  app's own California terms say so. `'Supervised'` is reportage, same as every other city.
- The CPUC has said on the record that this is a Transportation Charter Party (limo-class)
  permit, not an autonomous-vehicle one — real "hostile regulator" texture, not invented.
- The service is still invite-only (Tesla owners, employees, friends and family) — hence
  fleetCap 12, the smallest in the game, for the largest metro modeled so far.
- SFO joined the real service map on 21 July 2026 — six days before this shipped — making SF
  the first city where the airport run is real and current, rather than excluded (Miami,
  Orlando) or simply absent everywhere else.
- PG&E's EV2-A time-of-use tariff: 62c/kWh peak (4-9pm), 31c/kWh off-peak. Its off-peak price
  alone ties Dallas's peak and beats every other city's peak outright — there is no cheap hour
  here the way there is everywhere else. This is the standout real number and the reason
  `power` needed CITIES.sf's own comment rather than just new digits.
- insK 1.40 and incK 1.35 are the highest in the game — CA autonomous-adjacent insurance and
  SF's hills/traffic, respectively.

SCOPE: models San Francisco proper, not Tesla's whole published Bay Area (Golden Gate to San
Jose) — the same "one real slice of a bigger real area" move Dallas (downtown/Uptown out of
the whole Metroplex) and Tampa (the core out of the whole Bay) already made.

WHAT SHIPPED

- CITIES.sf: permit Supervised, fleetCap 12, fareK 1.15, depK 1.20, insK 1.40, incK 1.35, order
  5, needs shift1@orlando, PG&E power schedule, goal $38,000.
- ZONES_BY_CITY.sf: 13 zones — Financial District/SoMa/Mission live by default (work/work/night,
  same mix as Austin/Dallas/Tampa), Union Square/Marina/Castro/Haight/Richmond/Sunset opt-in,
  SFO (real, current, opt-in like every other city's airport), and three real, currently-
  operating taprooms (Cellarmaker, Fort Point Valencia, Woods Beer & Wine) — checked rather than
  assumed, since SF's beer scene has had real closures lately (21st Amendment, Black Sands,
  Fort Point's own Presidio taproom winding down).
- CHARGERS_BY_CITY.sf: three real sites, transcribed from Tesla's pages — Geary Boulevard (34
  stalls, 250 kW, the only fast site in the city and it's in the Richmond, not downtown), Van
  Ness Avenue (12 stalls, 72 kW, close to downtown and the weakest hardware), South San
  Francisco (20 stalls, 72 kW, near SFO). First city with no clean "fast and far or slow and
  close" answer — the close option and the airport option are both the weak hardware.
- New fog-green tone (day #187A4A 5.36:1, night #1B8250 4.82:1) rather than reclaiming the
  violet citiesplan.md had reserved for SF — Orlando already shipped with it, and reopening a
  live city's colour over a planning mistake was worse than picking a new hue.
- DESIGN.md's city table and citiesplan.md updated: the "blocked on Act 2" reasoning is struck
  through and corrected in place, same convention as the three earlier reportage corrections
  (Dallas's geofence, Miami's premise, Tampa/Orlando's tourism guess) — now four.

LEFT OPEN

`ROADS_BY_CITY.sf` is `{}`. This build environment has no route to the public OSRM demo server
bake-roads.js depends on (confirmed: 117/117 pairs failed to fetch), so SF draws straight-line
map fallbacks until `node deploy/scripts/bake-roads.js sf --write` is run somewhere with network
access. This is a fully supported state per the comment above roadsFor() — it affects map
drawing only, never fares, energy, or distance, which read dist() on straight lines regardless.

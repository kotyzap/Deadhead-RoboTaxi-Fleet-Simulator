0.58.0 — the splash keeps its aspect ratio, and becomes the intro's backdrop

WHAT WAS WRONG

Three asks from Pavel (2026-07-30), all about the 0.56.0 splash screen:

  1. "We need to ensure proper aspect ratio for ultra wide or wider display...
     maybe blur the sides like in 'Resume operations' and display full height
     and width accordingly not to hide the Title." The splash painted one
     background layer at `center/cover`, which on a 21:9 display crops the
     top and bottom — and the DEADHEAD wordmark lives at the top, so the
     title was the first thing to go.
  2. "Move 'loading ....' to the center and make it bigger." It was
     bottom-anchored (`align-items:flex-end` + `margin-bottom:9vh`) at 13px,
     which on the wide crop sat it over roadway detail and read as small.
  3. "How about bring the video and account signing-in and or registration or
     skip to the splash screen after 'loading'. That black screen is ugly.
     Let's merge loading and video and account together. Loading first
     followed by the video tutorial." The splash faded out to reveal #intro,
     whose own near-opaque radial gradient meant the video and the account
     card appeared on a black field.

WHAT CHANGED

Aspect ratio: two background layers instead of one. `.splash-fill` is the
same image at `center/cover`, blurred (28px), dimmed and scaled 1.12 to hide
blur()'s transparent edge; `.splash-bg` sits on top at `center/contain`, so
the artwork is never cropped at any aspect ratio and the letterbox space
reads as an out-of-focus continuation of it rather than as black bars. Same
blurred-surround idea `.modal` already uses behind "Resume operations", just
sourced from the image instead of a flat colour. The `orientation:portrait`
swap now targets both layers, not just the sharp one.

Loading pill: centred (`align-items:center`, the vh margin gone), 13px→17px,
dots 5px→7px, more padding and letter-spacing, plus a drop shadow so it holds
up over a bright sunset. A `max-width:760px` tier scales it back down so it
doesn't dominate a phone.

The merge — the splash now has two phases rather than one:

  PHASE 1, LOADING: unchanged. A fixed 4s timer, not a promise tied to boot,
  covering the sync render() and the async bootResume() chain both.

  PHASE 2, BACKDROP: on a FRESH run the splash does not fade out at all. It
  drops from z-index 200 to 80 — below #intro's 90, still above .app — and
  hides only its loading pill. Same painted element throughout, so there is
  no reload and no flash between phases. #intro gains `.intro-onsplash`,
  which swaps its near-opaque gradient for a light scrim plus
  backdrop-filter, gives .intro-frame a frosted plate to keep the video and
  account card readable over a photograph, and blurs the wings harder. On a
  RETURNING player (an autosave exists, so #resume shows and there is no
  #intro) it fades out exactly as before.

splashDone() decides which of those two by ASKING whether #intro is up
rather than predicting it, and showIntro() calls splashBackdrop() itself for
the case where the intro opens after the timer already ran — so both
orderings converge on the same screen. closeIntro() calls splashHide() on
every one of the intro's three exits (Skip, account created, signed-in
resume), and newFleet()'s non-tutorial branch does too, so a second city can
never inherit a stale backdrop over the live console.

The account card is injected and styled by cloud.js against the theme
tokens, so instead of duplicating a dozen of its rules at higher specificity
— which would rot the moment cloud.js changed one — `#intro.intro-onsplash
.intro-acct` REDEFINES those tokens (--c-card, --inset, --brd, --text, …)
for that subtree only. Custom properties inherit, so every cloud.js rule
underneath picks up dark-glass values on its own.

backdrop-filter only, never filter:, on #intro itself — a filter on an
ancestor creates a containing block and breaks descendant position:fixed
(#ray, #intro, #dimscrim all depend on the viewport). The test asserts this.

TESTS

test/splash-screen.test.js extended: the fill layer exists and uses
cover+blur while .splash-bg uses contain and never cover; the portrait swap
covers both layers; the pill is centred and no longer vh-pinned; .splash-back
drops below #intro and hides the pill; #intro's over-artwork variant exists
and uses backdrop-filter but not filter; the account card gets dark tokens;
and the live handover — splashBackdrop() un-hides + marks both elements and
cancels an in-flight fade, splashHide() takes both back down, splashDone()
backs the intro when it is open and hides fully when it is not.

Verified visually at 2560x1080 (ultrawide), 1440x900 and 393x852: full title
visible with blurred surround in every shape, and video + sign-in + skip all
sitting on the artwork instead of a black screen.

Full suite passes clean, and check-parity is green — deploy/public/index.html
is byte-identical to deadhead.html.
